Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

Extension: Sci-Tech Formula & Unit Companion

1. Information We Collect

The Sci-Tech Formula & Unit Companion extension is designed to function entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any personal data, telemetry, or usage metrics to external servers.

2. Local Storage

The extension requires the standard storage permission exclusively to save your user preferences locally on your personal device[cite: 5]. This includes:

  • Your saved formulas and constants
  • Your selected UI theme (Light/Dark mode)
  • Your recent unit conversion history

This data never leaves your browser and is strictly inaccessible to the developer or any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension does not utilize any third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or external APIs.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official developer portal: iunitconverters.net.
